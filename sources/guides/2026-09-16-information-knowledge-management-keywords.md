## A-B-C-D

- **Access Control, Authorization:** a technique that regulates who can access and interact with
  documents, to ensure security and confidentiality.
- **Action items:** activities agreed to be done as a result of a discussion — usually recorded in the
  meeting minutes.
- **Agenda:** a list of topics to be discussed, typically at a meeting or event.
- **Archive:** documents preserved and stored for reference, research, or historical purposes.
- **Backup:** a copy of data that can be used when the original data is unavailable.
- **Classification:** the process of assigning a document to relevant categories for easy management
  and analysis.
- **Cloud Storage:** when data is stored on a server, permitting users to access it through the
  Internet.
- **Data:** facts and records collected for reference or analysis.
- **Data Analysis:** examining raw data to uncover patterns, draw conclusions, and make informed
  decisions or recommendations.

## D-E-F-I

- **Database:** any collection of data or information specially organized for rapid search and
  retrieval by a computer.
- **Documentation:** the process of creating written or electronic records to describe procedures,
  specifications, or information.
- **Document Management:** a system or process used to capture, track, and store documents.
- **Explicit knowledge, Codified knowledge:** knowledge that can be captured using words, pictures, or
  numbers.
- **Facilitator:** someone who encourages knowledge sharing and decision-making during a meeting.
- **Information:** processed data that provides meaning, context, or understanding of the fact.
- **Information radiator, Visual control, Big Visible Charts:** a visible, physical display that
  provides instant information about the project without needing to ask or disturb the team.
- **Information retrieval:** finding relevant information or a document from a large repository, such
  as a database or an archive.
- **Information Security:** identifying, assessing, and managing information security risks.

## K-L-M

- **Kanban board:** a visual tool used to track and manage work in progress, typically organized into
  columns representing different stages of workflow.
- **Kick-off meeting:** takes place at the start of the project or phase to communicate the set
  objectives and gain the commitment of stakeholders.
- **Knowledge:** information and skills acquired through experience or education.
- **Knowledge Management:** the process of creating and applying knowledge to improve outcomes.
- **Learning and Development (L&D) Manager:** a professional responsible for designing and implementing
  programs to enhance the knowledge, skills, and capabilities of employees within an organization.
- **Lessons learned:** the knowledge gained during the current project to improve future performance.
- **Lessons learned meeting:** a meeting used to identify and share the knowledge gained during a
  project, phase, or iteration.
- **Lessons learned register:** used to record knowledge gained during a project, phase, or iteration so
  it can be used to improve future performance.
- **Meeting minutes:** a written record of all information presented and discussed during a meeting.

## N-P-R-S-T-V

- **Naming convention:** a set of rules and guidelines used to create significant and consistent names
  for files or variables.
- **Project Librarian, Project data administrator:** a professional responsible for managing information
  and documents in a project.
- **Project Management Information System (PMIS):** a software tool or system used to facilitate
  project planning, monitoring, and control by providing centralized access to project-related
  information and resources.
- **Records Management, Records Keeping:** the process of creating, storing, and maintaining project
  records.
- **Recovery:** restoring the backup data to replace lost or damaged original data.
- **Report:** a formal document that presents information, findings, analysis, or recommendations on a
  specific topic or issue.
- **Status Report:** a document that reports on the current status of the project, including
  information about cost and schedule performance.
- **Tacit Knowledge:** knowledge that can be difficult to articulate and share, such as beliefs,
  experience, and insights.
- **Version:** a specific iteration or variant of a product, software, or document — later versions
  typically expand on earlier versions.

## V

- **Version Control:** the application of numerical identifiers to track changes to files or
  deliverables, allowing team members to manage and collaborate on different versions effectively.
