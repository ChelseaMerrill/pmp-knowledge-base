# Project Stakeholders

Stakeholders are individuals, groups, or organizations that have an interest in the project or are
affected by its outcomes. Effective communication with stakeholders is critical to the success of the
project.

Diagram: "Anyone Involved or Impacted by the Project" sits at the center, surrounded by eight
stakeholder categories: Project Team, Project Manager, Sponsor, End-users, Suppliers, Government,
Financial Institutions, Others.

Here is an example of stakeholders involved in a construction project:

1. Project Team:
   - Architects & Designers
   - Engineers
   - Foreman
   - Labor
2. Project Manager:
   - Project Manager
   - Superintendent
   - Team manager
3. Sponsor:
   - Customer
   - Client
   - Owner
4. Users:
   - Homeowner
   - Tenant
5. Suppliers:
   - General Contractors
   - Providers & Vendors
   - Subcontractors
   - Legal Advisors
6. Government
   - Local authorities
   - Regulators
7. Financial Institutions:
   - Banks
   - Venture Capital firms
   - Business Angels
8. Others:
   - Neighbors
   - Family Members

By identifying and engaging stakeholders effectively, projects can minimize risks, avoid delays, and
ensure successful outcomes.
