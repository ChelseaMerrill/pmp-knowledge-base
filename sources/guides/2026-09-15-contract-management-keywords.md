## A-B-C

- **Acquisition:** the process of getting a product or service through a contract.
- **Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR):** a set of methods (mediation, arbitration) used to resolve
  conflicts and disputes outside the courtroom litigation process.
- **Arbitration:** involves a neutral third party (an arbitrator) who listens to both sides of a dispute
  and makes a binding decision.
- **Breach of Contract:** failure by either the owner or contractor, without legal excuse, to perform
  their obligations to the other party.
- **Claims management, Claims administration:** the process of assessing and resolving claims, ensuring
  compliance with contract terms.
- **Contract:** a mutually binding agreement that obligates the seller to provide the specified product
  or service and obligates the buyer to pay for it.
- **Contract administrator, Contract manager:** responsible for overseeing all aspects of contract
  preparation and administration.
- **Contract Termination:** action by the owner, under the terms of the contract, to end in whole or in
  part the work of the contract.
- **Contract Terms and Conditions:** the stated legal, financial, and administrative aspects of a
  contract.

## C-F-G

- **Cost Plus a Fee contract, Cost-Reimbursable contract:** an agreement where the contractor is paid for
  all legitimate costs incurred during the project, plus an additional fee representing contractor
  profit.
- **Cost Plus Award Fee contract:** the seller is reimbursed for all legitimate costs and is awarded an
  additional fee based on satisfaction of specific performance criteria set by the client.
- **Cost Plus Fixed Fee contract:** the seller is reimbursed for all legitimate costs of performing the
  contract work and receives a fixed-fee payment.
- **Cost Plus Incentive Fee contract:** the seller is reimbursed for all legitimate costs and earns an
  additional incentive fee based on meeting predefined performance targets.
- **Fixed Price contract, lump-sum contract:** the buyer pays the seller a set amount as defined by the
  contract, regardless of the incurred costs.
- **Fixed Price Incentive Fee contract:** the buyer pays the seller a set amount, and the seller can earn
  an additional amount if defined performance criteria are met.
- **Fixed Price with Economic Price Adjustments contract:** a fixed-price contract with a special
  provision allowing price adjustments due to changed conditions, such as inflation or cost changes for
  specific commodities.
- **Force majeure, Acts of God:** unforeseeable circumstances or events beyond the control of parties in
  a contract, such as natural disasters or wars.
- **Guaranteed Maximum Price contract:** sets a maximum price for a construction project, beyond which the
  contractor pays for the additional costs.

## I-L-M-N

- **Incentive:** a payment or concession to stimulate greater output or performance.
- **Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ):** used for acquiring an indefinite quantity of goods
  or services, with a stated lower and upper limit, within a fixed time period.
- **Letter of Intent:** a letter issued to a contractor to confirm the award of a contract pending the
  signing of formal contract documents.
- **Litigation:** the process of taking legal action.
- **Mediation:** a neutral third party (a mediator) assists disputing parties in reaching a mutually
  acceptable agreement.
- **Memorandum of Agreement (MOA):** a document describing the background, assumptions, and agreements
  between two parties.
- **Memorandum of Understanding (MOU):** a document describing an agreement for a cooperative effort
  between two parties.
- **Negligence:** failure to exercise reasonable care or caution that results in loss to the other party,
  through action or inaction.
- **Negotiation:** a direct discussion between parties involved in a dispute, attempting to find a
  mutually agreeable solution.

## P-R-S-T-U

- **Penalty:** a fine applied in case of non-fulfillment of contractual obligations.
- **Retainage, Holdback:** a portion of a contract payment held until contract completion, to ensure full
  performance of the contract terms.
- **Service Level Agreement (SLA):** an agreement on the level of service to be provided.
- **Special Conditions:** a section of contract documents setting out any special requirements or
  conditions specific to the contract at hand.
- **Standard Form of Contract (SFoC):** a pre-written, standardized contract document that streamlines
  contracting by providing consistent terms and conditions.
- **Target Cost:** a goal set for the final total cost, which the project team should aim to keep within.
- **Termination:** action by the owner, under the terms of the contract, to end in whole or in part the
  work of the contract.
- **Time and Material contract (T&M):** establishes a fixed rate, but not a precise statement of work.
- **Unit Price Contract:** a contract where the supplier agrees to provide goods or services at unit
  rates, and the final price depends on the quantities needed to carry out the work.

## W

- **Wage:** money paid as compensation for labor or services, usually according to contract, on an
  hourly, daily, or piecework basis.
- **Waiver:** a voluntary abandonment of a legal right or claim by one party, relieving the other party
  from their obligations.
