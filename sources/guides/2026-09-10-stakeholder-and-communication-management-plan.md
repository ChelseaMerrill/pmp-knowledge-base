# Stakeholder and Communication Management Plan

## 1. Stakeholder Management Plan

A Stakeholder Management Plan is a project document that guides how stakeholders will be identified,
analyzed, prioritized, engaged, and monitored throughout the project. It ensures systematic
engagement, improves project success, and clarifies responsibilities for managing stakeholder
interactions.

The purpose of the Stakeholder Management Plan is to provide a formal framework for managing
stakeholder relationships throughout the project. It ensures that all stakeholders are appropriately
considered, their expectations are understood, and their influence on the project is recognized.

## 2. Communication Strategy

A communication strategy is the structured, high-level blueprint for managing information flow in a
project. It defines the overall approach for engaging stakeholders, ensuring that communication
efforts are purposeful, consistent, and aligned with the project's vision and constraints.

## 3. Communication Management Plan

A Communication Management Plan, also known as Communication Plan, is a detailed tactical document
that defines how project communications will be managed throughout the project lifecycle. It
specifies the methods, timelines, and tools to ensure that relevant information reaches the right
stakeholders in a timely and effective manner.

The Communication Management Plan centers around three core questions: Who, What, and How.

- **Who:** identifies the target audience, including key stakeholders, executives such as the CEO,
  and other stakeholder groups, while considering their expectations and interests.
- **What:** defines the types of messages to be shared and the formats to be used, such as project
  status reports, dashboards, Excel sheets, or digital and paper-based updates.
- **How:** outlines the delivery method, the communication tools (e.g., email, meetings), the level of
  formality, and the frequency of updates, whether weekly, monthly, or as needed.

A well-structured Communication Management Plan provides an actionable guide for day-to-day
communication activities. It includes detailed formats and tools to be used, defines the frequency of
messages, and ensures that communication is consistent even when the project status remains stable.
