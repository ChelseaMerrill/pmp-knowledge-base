## A-B

- **Advance payment:** a prepaid expense from the client, paid to a vendor in advance for goods or
  services — the remaining amount is paid after successful project delivery.
- **Alliance, Partnering, Consortium:** a formal agreement between two or more parties to collaborate on
  a bid.
- **Bargaining:** the process of negotiating the terms and conditions of a transaction or agreement
  between two or more parties to reach a mutually acceptable outcome.
- **Benchmarking:** comparing actual or planned products, processes, and practices to those of comparable
  organizations, to identify best practices, generate improvement ideas, and provide a basis for
  measuring performance.
- **Bid, Tender:** the process by which suppliers or contractors submit offers to compete for a project
  or contract.
- **Bid Bond:** a bond covering a bid.
- **Bidder conference, contractor conference, vendor conference, Pre-Bid Conference:** a meeting held by
  an organization inviting potential suppliers to discuss project requirements, clarify ambiguities, and
  answer questions before they submit bids.
- **Bid Evaluation:** a comparison of bids received, including an assessment of each bid's validity
  against the tendering requirements.
- **Bills of Materials:** the complete set of physical elements required to accomplish a certain
  deliverable or the whole project.

## B-C

- **Bribe:** a reward or gift intended to change a decision or course of action in one's favor — a
  corrupt practice.
- **Bureaucracy:** a centralized system of administration for the conduct of business, usually highly
  structured and constrained by regulations, protocols, policies, and procedures.
- **Buyer, Client, Customer:** the party that purchases products, goods, or services.
- **Bypassing:** skipping or going around the chain of command in an organizational structure.
- **Centralized Procurement:** a system in which one department manages purchasing of goods and services
  for the entire organization.
- **Concession:** the official acceptance of components, materials, or workmanship that do not meet the
  exact specified requirements.
- **Conflict of interest:** a situation where a person or organization has a competing interest that
  could affect their judgment, decision, or behavior.
- **Contract Award:** the issuing and signing of contract documents by a contractor.
- **Control Procurements:** the process of managing procurement, monitoring its performance, making
  changes and corrections as appropriate, and closing out contracts.

## C-D-E-F

- **Credit:** deferred payment for purchased goods or services.
- **Decentralized Procurement:** allows individual stakeholders to make purchases for their own
  departments.
- **Delivery:** the transfer or handover of a product from one party to another.
- **Depreciation:** the decrease in the value of tangible property.
- **Double-stage Bidding, Two-stage Tendering:** a procurement process where initial proposals are
  solicited to outline the technical approach, followed by detailed bids from shortlisted candidates
  based on refined project specifications.
- **E-tendering, eProcurement:** using online procurement platforms to solicit bids and evaluate
  suppliers automatically.
- **Evaluation criteria, source selection criteria:** criteria used to evaluate proposals in order to
  select the best supplier.
- **Exit criteria:** conditions to be fulfilled in order for a seller's proposal to be considered in the
  first place — otherwise it gets rejected outright.
- **Feasibility study:** an assessment conducted to evaluate the practicality, viability, and potential
  success of a proposed project or business venture.

## I-M

- **Fee:** the amount of money paid to a provider for their services, typically in addition to any
  reimbursed costs.
- **Inflation:** the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises.
- **In-house:** work undertaken by the organization's own employees rather than by outside contract.
- **Integrity:** the quality of being honest, ethical, and morally upright.
- **Intellectual Property Rights:** exclusive rights granted for a specified period to inventors,
  artists, writers, etc.
- **Inventory, stock:** an itemized list of the materials on hand.
- **Invitation for Bid, Request for Tender, Invitation to Tender:** a document issued by an entity
  inviting suppliers or contractors to bid on a project or service.
- **Invoice:** a contractor's bill or written request for payment upon delivering a product or service.
- **Make-or-buy analysis:** used to determine whether to produce goods or services internally (make) or
  outsource them from external suppliers (buy).

## M-N-O

- **Market Analysis, Market Survey:** a set of activities to determine what is available in the
  marketplace.
- **Negotiated tendering:** the buyer approaches a single supplier and asks them to deliver the work.
- **Networking:** the exchange of information among individuals or organizations, aiming to build
  relationships for future collaboration.
- **Notice of Change:** formal, written communication detailing modifications or updates to a product,
  process, or system.
- **Notice of Termination:** a written notice to the contractor that the contract is being terminated
  for convenience or default.
- **Notice to Proceed:** formal notification to the supplier requesting the start of work.
- **Oligopoly:** a market with few sellers, where one seller's action affects other sellers' prices.
- **Open tendering:** a public announcement inviting contractors to submit proposals or bids for a
  project.
- **Outsourcing:** hiring a party outside a company to perform services or create goods traditionally
  performed in-house.

## P-Q-R

- **Procurement, purchasing, supply chain:** the act of obtaining goods, supplies, and/or services.
- **Procurement Audit:** verifies whether the procurement processes were followed.
- **Procurement Inspection:** verifies whether procured goods or services conform to contract
  requirements.
- **Procurement Management Plan:** establishes how the project team will acquire goods and services from
  suppliers.
- **Procurement Performance Evaluation:** a comprehensive review of conducted procurements to avoid
  pitfalls in the future.
- **Procurement Strategy:** a macro view of the approach to securing the necessary physical resources to
  perform the project.
- **Proposal:** prepared by the seller in response to a procurement request from the buyer.
- **Purchase Order (PO):** a standard document used to obtain supplies and non-personnel services when
  the total amount is relatively small.
- **Quote, quotation:** a statement of price in response to a request for goods or services.

## R-S

- **Request For Information (RFI):** used when more information on the goods/services to be acquired is
  needed from sellers.
- **Request For Proposal (RFP):** used when the required solution, product, or service isn't easy to
  determine.
- **Request For Quotation (RFQ):** used when more information is needed on how vendors would satisfy
  requirements and how much it would cost.
- **Sampling:** selecting a representative part of a population for inspection or analysis, to determine
  characteristics of the whole population.
- **Screening system:** a technique used to filter proposals based on specific criteria.
- **Selective tendering, Restricted tendering:** a bidding process where only certain (personally
  invited) vendors can bid for the project.
- **Single source:** obtaining a product or service from only one supplier even though many vendors are
  available in the market.
- **Single-stage bidding:** all potential bidders submit their proposals in one phase, after which bids
  are evaluated and the contract is awarded.
- **Site walkthrough:** physical inspection of a construction site.

## S-T-U-V-W

- **Sole source:** only one provider in the market could fulfill the requirements of the procurement.
- **Statement Of Work (SOW):** provides sellers with a clearly stated set of goals, requirements, and
  outcomes from which they can provide a quantifiable response.
- **Supplier, Vendor, Seller, Contractor, Subcontractor:** an individual or entity that provides goods or
  services in exchange for payment.
- **Trial engagement:** engaging a provider for a small portion of work to assess performance before
  awarding the full project.
- **Unsolicited proposal:** a proposal submitted that is not a response to a formal solicitation.
- **Vendor lock-in:** a situation where a customer becomes dependent on a particular vendor for their
  products or services, making it difficult to switch to another vendor.
- **Walk-away point:** when a party feels there's no point in continuing negotiations or pursuing a deal.
- **Weighting system, scoring model:** a set of weighted criteria and corresponding key indicators to
  measure and score components for comparison and prioritization.
