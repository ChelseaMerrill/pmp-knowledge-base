# Project Governance

## 1. Definition

Project governance is the framework of policies, processes, and practices that guide project
decision-making, ensuring alignment with organizational objectives, accountability, and successful
project delivery.

## 2. Effective project governance

Project governance ensures that the project is well-structured, aligned with organizational
objectives, effectively managed, and compliant with established standards and regulations.

Diagram: "Project governance is the framework of" Policies, Processes, Responsibilities.

Five pillars of effective project governance:
1. Clear Roles and Accountability
2. Decision-Making Framework
3. Effective Risk Management
4. Performance Monitoring and Control (Oversight)
5. Stakeholder Engagement & Transparency

## 3. Project governance example

An example of project governance, in the construction of a new airport terminal, is the establishment
of a steering committee to oversee the project to ensure it aligns with government regulations, budget
constraints, stakeholder expectations, and environmental standards. Governance structures define
decision-making processes, risk management protocols, and performance monitoring to keep the project
on track.

The following is an example of a project organigram that represents the hierarchy, roles, and
decision-making authority, ensuring clear accountability, structured governance, and alignment with
project objectives.

**Project Board** (oversight level): Finance board · Design authority · Regulatory authority ·
Government group · Community stakeholder group · Procurement board

**Project Team** (delivery level): Design/engineering team · Construction team 1 · Construction team 2
· Technical team 1 · Technical team 2
