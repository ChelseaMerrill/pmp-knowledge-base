# Key Contract Clauses

Contracts are legally binding agreements that define the rights, responsibilities, and obligations of
the parties involved. Below are key contract clauses commonly found in various types of contracts:

- **Payment clause:** the payment process — timeframes, conditions, required documents, approval
  process, etc. — and the final payment terms.
- **Change clause:** what could be changed; defines the change order process; defines how the new price
  will be established.
- **Indemnity clause:** transfers risk — indemnifies a party to secure them against loss or damage;
  defines the events and processes that trigger indemnities.
- **Liquidated damages:** a defined amount to be paid in case of damages (e.g. delays) — a substitute for
  proving actual damages.
- **Dispute resolution:** how to resolve claims without litigation; defines the arbitration process and
  when to go to court.
- **Time-barred clause:** sets deadlines for claims — beyond the deadline, the contractor's claims are
  not admissible.
- **Termination clause:** defines when the contract should be terminated; defines the termination
  process.
