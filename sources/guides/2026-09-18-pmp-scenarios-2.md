## Scenario 21

**Situation:** The PM noticed that there were constant disagreements between team members during
meetings.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should intervene to resolve these disagreements and guide the team towards a culture of
  collaboration and cooperation.

## Scenario 22

**Situation:** A Kanban framework was chosen to implement the project.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should focus on continuous workflow and limit Work In Progress (WIP).

## Scenario 23

**Situation:** A senior executive criticized the project's output.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should analyze the reason for this criticism and take the appropriate corrective actions.

## Scenario 24

**Situation:** The supplier is having a hard time meeting quality requirements.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should provide the supplier with feedback to improve their performance.
- As a last resort, the PM should find another supplier.

## Scenario 25

**Situation:** A key team member left before the project was finalized.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should evaluate the impact on the project.
- The PM should ensure that knowledge transfer is performed.
- The PM should follow the resource management plan to find a replacement.

## Scenario 26

**Situation:** A team member refuses to participate in team meetings.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should talk to the member to understand the root cause of the problem.
- The PM should remind the team member of the importance of their participation according to the team
  charter.

## Scenario 27

**Situation:** Someone leaked confidential information about the project.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should investigate the incident and take disciplinary action as per company policies.

## Scenario 28

**Situation:** There was an accident in the work environment that resulted in an injury to one of the
team members.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should investigate the causes of the incident and develop a plan to avoid its recurrence, to
  ensure safety in the work environment.

## Scenario 29

**Situation:** The client wants to be involved in each step of the project development.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should adopt an agile approach to ensure the continuous involvement of the customer.

## Scenario 30

**Situation:** The PM is asked to plan for the new project before initiating its execution.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should choose a waterfall approach to create a well-defined plan including the scope,
  schedule, and budget before starting execution.

## Scenario 31

**Situation:** The PM is abruptly asked to terminate the project.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should formally close the project and perform all related activities.

## Scenario 32

**Situation:** The project team is going through a long storming stage.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should coach team members to create a culture of trust, transparency, and collaboration.

## Scenario 33

**Situation:** The Scrum project team keeps getting bigger, making communication and synchronization
harder.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should apply a Scrum of Scrums approach and divide the team into smaller teams.

## Scenario 34

**Situation:** The PM is managing a project using the Kanban framework and the sponsor wants an update
on the team's performance.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should show the sponsor the Kanban board for a visual display of the team's progress.

## Scenario 35

**Situation:** An Agile team is wondering how they're going to manage risks.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should guide the team on how to create a risk-adjusted backlog (a backlog that contains
  activities relating to managing risk, in addition to the user stories associated with delivering
  value).

## Scenario 36

**Situation:** The PM is evaluating two potential projects for execution and is hesitant about which to
choose.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should calculate both projects': Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), or
  Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR).
- The project with the higher score is the better choice.

## Scenario 37

**Situation:** The PM is asked to iterate the quantitative risk analysis outputs to find out the
probability of completing the project within the defined budget or cost.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should use the Monte Carlo analysis method to assess the impact of the risks.

## Scenario 38

**Situation:** In a team meeting, only a few team members were actively participating.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should use round-robin to ensure the participation of all team members.

## Scenario 39

**Situation:** The PM is following the project management plan, but stakeholders seem unhappy.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should meet the stakeholders and listen to their concerns.
- The PM should perform a root cause analysis behind the stakeholders' dissatisfaction.

## Scenario 40

**Situation:** The PM is facing resistance from key stakeholders.

**Course of action:**
- The PM should understand the reason behind the stakeholders' resistance.
- The PM should involve the key stakeholder.
- The PM should adjust the stakeholder engagement plan and the communication management plan.
