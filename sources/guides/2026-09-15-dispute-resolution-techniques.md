# Dispute Resolution Techniques

Dispute Resolution Techniques are methods used to resolve conflicts or disagreements between parties
fairly and efficiently. These techniques are essential in project management, construction, and other
areas where conflicts may arise.

Below is an overview of the most common dispute-resolution techniques, presented from least to most
formal:

1. **Negotiation:** a direct discussion between the parties involved in a dispute, where they attempt to
   find a mutually agreeable solution.
2. **Mediation:** a neutral third party, called a mediator, assists the disputing parties in reaching a
   mutually acceptable agreement.
3. **Adjudication:** an impartial third party reviews evidence and arguments to make a decision resolving
   a dispute.
4. **Expert determination:** an independent expert is appointed to resolve a dispute based on their
   specialized knowledge and expertise.
5. **Arbitration:** involves a neutral third party, known as an arbitrator, who listens to both sides of
   the dispute and makes a binding decision.
6. **Litigation:** the process of taking legal action.
